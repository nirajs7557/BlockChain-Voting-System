How to Use This Voting System

  1. Review the candidate information for Aman, Karan, Niraj, and Gopal
  2. Click on a candidate to select them (the selected candidate will be highlighted)
  3. Click the "Cast Vote" button to record your vote on the blockchain
  4. Watch as each vote is added as a new block to the blockchain
  5. See real-time election results update on the right side
